FAQ

What you're probably wondering

Do we need a Synology NAS? +
Yes — the system runs on Docker, so you need a Synology (or another NAS/server with Docker). If you don't have one, we'll help you pick a suitable model. The purchase price is usually lower than a year of a cloud CRM subscription.
We have no IT person. Can we handle it? +
You don't have to. We do the installation and configuration, migrate the data and train the team. The Pro bundle includes 3 months of support — in practice you contact us only a few times.
Is our data safe? +
The data physically stays on your NAS, not in a third-party cloud. Communication is encrypted with HTTPS and backups are encrypted and automatic. If you prefer, no remote access from the internet is enabled at all.
Can we try it before buying? +
Yes — we show a live demo on your own documents, before you order. You'll see invoicing, archive and CRM on real data.
What if it stops fitting us? +
The software is yours — no locked programs or closed licences. An export of your data is always yours; if you ever want to leave, you leave with your data. We never hold it "hostage".
