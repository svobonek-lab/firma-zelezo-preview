Questions
Frequently asked
Do we need a Synology NAS?

Yes, the system runs via Docker on Synology (or another NAS/server with Docker support). If you don't have one, we'll help you choose — the purchase price is far lower than a year of a typical cloud CRM subscription.

What if we have no IT person?

Installation is a single guided script — even the owner can handle it. The Pro bundle includes 3 months of onboarding support.

Is our data safe?

The data stays physically on your NAS. Nothing is copied to a third-party cloud. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS and backups are encrypted and automatic.

Can we try it first?

Yes — we're happy to show a live demo on your data before you order, even with your real documents.
