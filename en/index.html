Workflow

How it works

From an incoming document to a finished invoice – every step has its own automated process.

1

Document intake

Drop a PDF into the consume folder (over SMB) or upload it in the web app. The system picks it up in real time.

2

OCR & recognition

Paperless turns a scan into searchable text (Czech + English) and stores it in the database. Files are tagged automatically.

3

Invoicing

From the customer data in CRM you create an invoice in InvoiceNinja – with VAT, numbers and attachment.

4

Backup & monitoring

Duplicati backs up data daily, Uptime Kuma watches everything. You just focus on the work.
